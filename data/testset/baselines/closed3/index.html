Contact density binning instructions (C0–C3）

Currently Level 4：C0 contactless → C1 light/Instantaneous → C2 point/line of continuous contact → C3 high contact(formerly "large area/"Load-bearing" and "winding"/Confrontation" merger). Reason for merger: close-fitting load-bearing (hug/piggyback) versus fighting (fight/wrestle) is not strictly sorted in terms of contact area, But both are significantly higher than C0–C2; Forcibly splitting it into two levels can easily create a false "difficulty ladder".

  • C3 It still contains multiple interaction types; for fine-grained failure modes, please click board_cat（hug / fight ...) Separately.
  • main curve view C2→C3 and Δ(C3−C0): high contact relatively low/Whether the contact is degraded.

Mesh Anat / Inter detail

main table Anat = Anat_mesh，Inter = Inter_mesh。 The last three columns (P_extra / orphan / leftover) is a diagnostic quantity. Data source:hybrid:gemini-3-pro-image:recomputed_n=2469,gpt-image-2:recomputed_n=2078,seedream-5-pro:recomputed_n=2488。

How to calculate it (popular version)

common prefix

Multi-HMR After checking out the person, press prompt of R# number of people to do top-k（keep）。 Anat and Inter share the same group of people。

Anat_mesh（↑the better)

ask"N How much of the human body can a frame cover?"; how much of the body can't be covered? → leftover / orphan → s_extra, and then with the residual/structure/Number of people fidelity bonus points synthesis.

Anat ≈ 0.40 s_extra + 0.30 s_resid + 0.15 s_struct + 0.15 s_detect（s=1−P）

Inter_mesh（↑the better)

look at two people 3D: Whether the mold penetration and contact are consistent prompt Intention (to hold) / Don't touch / not specified).

Inter ≈ 0.55 s_pen + 0.45 s_prox（required；forbidden use s_clear；unspecified only s_pen）
